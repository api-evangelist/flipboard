<p>Flipboard was honored to host a meetup of the <a href="http://www.meetup.com/Palo-Alto-iOS-Developers/events/224782782/">Palo Alto iOS Developers</a> group about developing apps for watchOS 2 at our office in Palo Alto last week. <a href="https://twitter.com/benmorrow">Ben Morrow</a>, writer of the <a href="http://www.happy.watch/">Happy Watch</a> blog and host of several Apple Watch hackathons, gave a talk deep diving into various aspects of watchOS 2.
<!--break--></p>
